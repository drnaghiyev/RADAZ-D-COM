Interactive points are separate reference-plate overlays, never patient-image segmentation.
The heart overlay is available under CC BY-SA 3.0 and the endocrine overlay under
CC BY-SA 4.0, matching their source plates. Overlay author: RADAZ, 2026.
This licensing applies to those image/point contributions, not the application code.
Historical English labels remain in the original plates; names in the catalogue
are independently curated in Azerbaijani and Latin. The catalogue covers major
structures and selected parts, not all microscopic anatomy, variants or projections.
Hand scaphoid, triquetrum, trapezium, trapezoid and hamate use modern terminology.
